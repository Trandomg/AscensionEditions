## Ascension Editions
A Slay the Spire 2 mod that changes your cards' appearance.  
Cards gain a holographic effect upon clearing the final boss on Ascension 10 with it in your deck.  

<br> 

### Multiplayer
The mod can be used in multiplayer, however other players will not see your cards as holographic.  
You will see other players cards as holographic if __you__ have cleared Ascension 10 with it in your deck.  

<br> 

### Installation 
Create a folder named `mods` in the same directory as sts2.exe. Then unzip the contents of the release into that folder.
Final structure should look like `...\Slay the Spire 2\mods\AscensionEditions`
